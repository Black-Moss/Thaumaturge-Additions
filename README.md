# Thaumaturge Additions

An addon mod for [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/thaumaturge).

Adds the self-repairing Void Traveller's Boots, the Rainbow Scribing Tools, the Void Brain in a Jar, the Everburning Urn, the Focus Effect: Salis Mundus, and — the centerpiece of this mod — **Arcane Workbench upgrades for Traveler's Backpack / Sophisticated Backpacks**, letting you craft arcane recipes anywhere using the vis of the chunk you are standing in.

## Features

### Arcane Workbench Upgrades (Traveler's Backpack / Sophisticated Backpacks)

Each of the two backpack mods gets a matching pair of upgrades, with an identical workflow:

- **Arcane Workbench Upgrade**: installs a full arcane workbench inside your backpack. A 3x3 crafting grid, six primal crystal slots (Aer / Ignis / Aqua / Terra / Ordo / Perditio) and a wand slot, laid out roughly like the vanilla Arcane Workbench. It draws on the vis of **the chunk you are currently in**.
- **Arcane Charger Upgrade**: widens the vis drain from the single chunk underfoot to the **surrounding 3x3 chunks**, behaving just like the vanilla Arcane Workbench Charger. It has an on/off switch, so you can turn it off whenever you want to leave your local aura alone.

Requires **[Traveler's Backpack](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/travelers-backpack)** and/or **[Sophisticated Backpacks](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/sophisticated-backpacks)**. Each backpack mod is an independent optional dependency — install either one, or both.

### Void Traveller's Boots

An upgraded take on Traveler's Boots, built on Thaumaturge's void gear:

- **Self-repairing** — recharges itself from stored vis, so it will never truly break.
- **+6 blocks of safe-fall distance.**
- **Movement speed boost** (on foot and while wading through water), plus increased step height and jump strength.
- **5% vis discount** on everything you cast.
- **Dyeable**, just like ordinary traveller's boots.

### Rainbow Scribing Tools

No matter what dye you put in this little bottle, what comes out is always black. A textbook case of "gorgeous black".

### Void Brain in a Jar

The void version of a Brain in a Jar — in other words, once it is full, the excess is simply cleared away.

### Everburning Urn

A sibling of the Everfull Urn, except it produces lava instead of water. You can pipe lava in from just about any mod. Do keep in mind that it is far slower than its water counterpart, and it is not free — every bucket of lava costs roughly 25 vis. For safety's sake, automatic filling of nearby containers is disabled; molten lava should not splash everywhere on its own.

### Focus Effect: Salis Mundus

Salis Mundus in the shape of a proper focus. Cast it at a block that has a transmutation recipe and the block is converted — and you do not consume the Salis Mundus itself, which makes large-scale transmutation projects far less painful on your reserves.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 26.1.2
- NeoForge 26.1.2.114 or newer
- **[Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/thaumaturge)** 1.0.2 or newer (required)

Optional:

- **[Traveler's Backpack](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/travelers-backpack)** 11.2.8+ — for its Arcane Workbench and Charger upgrades
- **[Sophisticated Backpacks](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/sophisticated-backpacks)** 3.26.9+ — for its Arcane Workbench and Charger upgrades
